STATEMENT OF WORK
Project: Northstar Retail — Shopify Plus storefront
Client: Northstar Retail Ltd.
Date: 12 August 2026
Agency: Harbor & Co.

1. Scope of Work
Harbor & Co. will design and develop a Shopify Plus storefront for Northstar Retail including:
- Theme design in Figma (desktop + mobile) for Home, PLP, PDP, Cart, Checkout (Shopify checkout), About, Contact
- Development on Shopify Plus using Online Store 2.0
- Up to 12 product templates / metafield-driven PDP variants
- Collections: 8 collection templates
- Stripe payments (Shopify Payments)
- Klaviyo email integration (standard events: checkout started, order placed, abandoned cart)
- Content migration of up to 400 SKUs from the existing Magento catalog (CSV)
- Basic SEO: titles, metas, schema for product
- QA on Chrome, Safari, Firefox (latest) and iOS/Android Safari/Chrome
- Training: one 90-minute admin handoff

2. Explicitly out of scope
- Native iOS or Android applications
- Wholesale / B2B price lists and customer-specific catalogs
- ERP / NetSuite / SAP integrations
- Custom loyalty or points programs
- Marketplace integrations (Amazon, eBay)
- Headless storefront or Hydrogen
- Multilingual / multi-currency beyond USD
- Custom checkout extensibility beyond Shopify Checkout
- Photography, copywriting, brand identity

3. Assumptions
- Client provides Magento CSV export
- Brand guidelines exist and will be supplied in week 1
- One round of design revision, one round of development revision

4. Commercials
- Fixed fee: $48,000 USD
- Timeline: 10 weeks
- Blended rate for change orders: $165/hour
- Internal cost rate for margin math: $80/hour
- Change orders require written approval before work begins
